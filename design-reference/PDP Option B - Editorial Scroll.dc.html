Questions

Will it fit through my door?
It ships in four boxes, the largest 34" × 30" × 26", which clears a standard 30" doorway. Assembly happens in the room, so nothing oversized ever has to turn a corner.
How do the covers wash?
Unzip, machine wash cold on a gentle cycle, tumble dry low. Base covers come off too, which most modular couches do not allow.
Can I add pieces later?
Yes. Every module uses the same ModuleLock™ connector, so a corner or ottoman bought two years from now attaches to what you already own.
What if I don't like it?
Thirty nights to decide. If it is not right we arrange free pickup and refund in full, no restocking fee.
